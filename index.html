Leopards are large, powerful wild cats famous for their golden, spotted coats. Native to Africa and Asia, they are highly adaptable and can thrive in various environments, from savannas to dense forests. Leopards are solitary, stealthy hunters known for their incredible agility, speed, and ability to climb trees, where they often drag their prey to keep it safe from other predators.
Deer are graceful, herbivorous mammals found in forests, grasslands, and tundras around the world. They are best known for their impressive antlers, which are typically grown and shed annually by males. As agile and fast runners, deer rely on their keen senses of hearing and smell to avoid predators while foraging for grass, leaves, and shoots.
The American black bear is the smallest and most common bear species in North America. Highly adaptable, they inhabit forests, mountains, and swamps across the continent. They are opportunistic omnivores with a diet heavily reliant on plants, berries, and insects. Despite their name, their coats can range from black to brown, cinnamon, or even a rare white, and they are exceptionally skilled at climbing trees.
